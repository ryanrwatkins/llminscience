As a starting place, here are initial recommendations of questions to be asked when reviewing research in which an LLM was used as part of their scientific research workflow.

- Were [LLM agent(s)](https://python.langchain.com/en/latest/modules/agents.html) used in the research?
- Were the intermediate steps, and sequence, of the [LLM agent(s)](https://python.langchain.com/en/latest/modules/agents.html) described?
- Is the code for creating the [LLM agent(s)](https://python.langchain.com/en/latest/modules/agents.html) available?
