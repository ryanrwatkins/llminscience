LLMs stand for Large Language Models. They are a type of generative artificial intelligence (genAI) model that uses deep learning techniques to process and generate human-like text. LLMs are trained on massive amounts of data, such as text from the internet or other large text corpora, and can learn to understand and generate text in a wide range of styles and domains.

LLMs are capable of various natural language processing (NLP) tasks, including language generation, text completion, text classification, sentiment analysis, and more. They can be used in a wide range of applications, including scientific research, content generation, language translation, question answering, and chatbots, among others.
